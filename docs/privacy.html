Privacy Policy — openworld VPN

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This policy explains what openworld VPN (com.openworld.vpn) does with data. It describes the app's actual behaviour rather than a generic template.

Who runs this app

openworld VPN is published by an independent developer, tmsoft12. For any question about this policy, write to crazysoft394@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no account, no sign-up and no login. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not run any VPN server, and we operate no server that carries your traffic.
  • We do not log, store or analyse your browsing activity.
  • The app shows ads. The ad provider uses your device advertising identifier.
  • This is an access tool, not a privacy tool. Read the traffic section below before relying on it.

What the app does

The app connects to servers published by the VPN Gate academic project of the University of Tsukuba, Japan. These servers are personal computers run by volunteers around the world. They are not ours, and we have no relationship with the people who run them.

The list of available servers is fetched from a Google Firebase Firestore database that we maintain. That request is read-only: the app downloads the list and writes nothing back. No identifier of yours is sent with it.

Data collected by the app itself

None. The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting and no telemetry.

The following is stored only on your own device and never leaves it:

Stored itemPurpose
Cached server list Avoids re-downloading roughly 1.3 MB on every launch. Includes the measured response time of each server.
Consent flag Records that you accepted the notice shown before your first connection, so it is not shown again.

Uninstalling the app deletes both. Clearing the app storage in Android settings has the same effect.

Data collected by third parties

Unity Ads

Ads are served by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Unity receives your device advertising identifier along with technical device and network information, and uses it to select and measure ads. This is the only identifier associated with you that leaves the app.

The Unity Ads SDK is not started until you accept the notice shown before your first connection. If you never accept it, the SDK is never initialised and no advertising identifier is read.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in Android Settings, Privacy, Ads. Unity publishes its own policy at unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Google Firebase

The server list is hosted on Firebase Firestore. As with any internet request, Google infrastructure processes your IP address in order to deliver the response. We do not receive, store or have access to those addresses, and no user identifier is attached to the request.

VPN Gate

When you connect, your internet traffic passes through a VPN Gate volunteer server. The next section explains what that means.

What happens to your traffic when connected

While the VPN is on, all of your device internet traffic is routed through the volunteer server you selected.

  • The VPN Gate project states that it retains connection logs, including source IP addresses and connection timestamps.
  • The operator of each individual server can observe traffic passing through their own machine.
  • We have no control over either, and we cannot see, intercept or influence that traffic ourselves.

Do not use this app for anything you need to keep private. It is useful for reaching content that is blocked on your network, and for choosing a low-latency route. It is not an anonymity tool.

The same warning is shown inside the app before your first connection and remains visible on the main screen.

Children

This app is not directed at children and is rated for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Your choices

  • Declining the notice shown before the first connection stops both the VPN and the ad SDK from starting.
  • Resetting your advertising identifier in Android settings breaks the link between you and past ad activity.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Because we hold no account and no server-side record of you, there is nothing for us to delete on request. No data of yours is in our possession.
